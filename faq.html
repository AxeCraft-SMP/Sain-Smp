📜 FAQ & SERVER RULES

Everything you need to know before joining SAIN SMP.

🌍 SERVER INFORMATION

What is SAIN SMP?

SAIN SMP is a Minecraft Survival Multiplayer server where players can explore, build, fight and enjoy adventures together.

How can I join?

Open Minecraft, add our server IP and join the adventure!

What game mode do we offer?

Our main game mode is Survival SMP.

Where can I contact the staff?

Join our Discord server to contact the staff team and get help.

⚖️ SERVER RULES

01. Respect Everyone

Respect all players and staff. Bullying, harassment and discrimination are not allowed.

02. No Cheating

Hacks, unfair cheats and unauthorized advantages are forbidden.

03. No Griefing

Do not destroy, steal or damage other players' builds without permission.

04. No Scamming

Do not scam other players during trades or deals.

05. No Spam

Avoid repeated messages, chat flooding and unnecessary advertising.

06. No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches or server exploits for unfair advantages.

07. Follow Staff Instructions

Listen to staff members and follow their instructions.

08. No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be an owner, staff member or another player.

09. No Advertising

Advertising other servers or communities without permission is forbidden.

10. Have Fun!

Enjoy your adventure and help keep SAIN SMP a friendly community!

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What happens if I break a rule?

You may receive a warning, mute, temporary ban or permanent ban depending on the violation.

Can I report a player?

Yes! Contact our staff team through Discord and provide evidence.

Can I invite my friends?

Of course! Invite your friends and enjoy playing together.
